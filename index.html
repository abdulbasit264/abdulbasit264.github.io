02 — Flagship

A logistics company's whole back office, in one platform.

Built for GlobalGates Logistics: it started as an AI assistant for shipment emails and grew into a multi-tenant CRM + ERP + HRM that runs the business.

Laravel 13LivewirePostgreSQLRedisLLMsDocker

Logistics CRM + ERP + HRM SaaS

Every tenant gets its own database. Air waybills arrive as PDFs or photos; a vision LLM extracts them and drafts the notification to the freight agent. Invoices, payments, commissions and payroll all post through a single double-entry ledger that can prove it hasn't been tampered with.

AI document parsing

Vision LLMs read air waybills from PDFs and phone photos, then draft the shipment email. Every paid AI call has a per-tenant daily cap and degrades to manual, never to an error.

Tamper-evident ledger

Exact integer money, a SHA-256 hash chain across journal entries, a truncation anchor, and nightly reconciliation of every control account against its sub-ledger.

Full ERP

Invoicing, AR/AP, multi-currency, commissions, inventory, multi-entity consolidation and CEO reporting, all posting through one ledger.

CRM + HRM

Leads, pipeline and lists alongside employees, leave, attendance and payroll, with field-level change history on every record.

Security by design

Database-per-tenant isolation, SSO/SCIM, role and row-level access, encrypted secrets with key rotation, and GDPR erasure that anonymises the history too.

Operated, not just built

One-command idempotent deploys, fleet schema health checks, point-in-time recovery with monthly restore drills, and a REST API with idempotency keys plus an MCP server.
